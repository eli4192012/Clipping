# v5.35 validation · Public YouTube channel watching

## Implemented behavior

- **YouTube channels** connects a public source channel or @handle without OAuth/API keys. The default is the Colts channel and Automatic per upload; fixed Interview, Podcast, football-highlight and saved-project settings remain available.
- A detached, elected watcher polls about every five minutes. It examines the latest 50 regular uploads from the Videos tab, validates individual publication times and channel ownership, and queues sources from the last 48 hours or the newest finished upload even if older. Live/upcoming/still-processing videos wait; unknown dates and unavailable videos report errors and retry. Channel failures back off to at most one hour.
- Imports are durable queue entries before any download. Download, local transcription, selection and captioned exports run serially under the existing heavy-job lock. Publication eligibility is checked again at execution; expired non-newest sources are skipped and the queue continues.
- Automatic mode gives the full saved transcript to the existing title/speech/face detector, then retains its confidence and resolved per-video settings. It does not transcribe samples again, including for an empty transcript. Interview and Podcast rules remain distinct; Sports use 10–35 seconds and available football vision. Original speech duration preferences survive a retry after Sports detection.
- Video IDs deduplicate across completed, failed, removed and interrupted automatic jobs, including a crash between the watcher and queue receipts. Matching manual queue jobs also count as handled. Settings for waiting jobs stay saved; reconnecting affects future jobs. Explicitly paused queues stay paused, while naturally emptied queues restart for new arrivals.
- Watch pause/resume, Check now, last-check/error/history display and queue navigation are available. Browser closure does not stop detached workers; saved enabled watches restart when the app reopens after shutdown. Normal watching holds a temporary idle-sleep assertion. No social publication is triggered.

## Preservation and limits

The pre-change inventory covered **2,409 existing data/export/model files** by size and modification time. None disappeared. Only `data/clipping-queue.sqlite3` changed, for the backward-compatible queue schema. Existing source videos, transcripts, exports, model weights, posting-style corpus and manual publishing text were unchanged. The channel database/lock are new local runtime files and are excluded from Git. Major remains 5, minor is 35; analysis-cache `modes.VERSION` remains 10.

The live production channel has not been connected automatically; choose the source/preferences and click Connect and start watching to enable it. An actual future-upload-to-new-AI-clips unattended trial remains outstanding. Automated fixture tests and a live metadata lookup do not establish that future YouTube changes, bot checks or network interruptions will never block imports. The latest-50 discovery window can miss older eligible uploads on unusually busy channels or after a long outage. Detection is fallible, and sports edits remain visual drafts requiring review.

The Mac must be powered on, awake and online. Normal idle-sleep prevention does not defeat lid sleep, manual sleep or shutdown. The queue's optional Amphetamine session ends when that queue ends; it does not provide continuous watching while idle with the lid closed. Physical closed-lid operation remains unverified. This release automatically clips/exports; the future automatic-publishing workflow and two-posts-per-hour limit are not implemented here.
